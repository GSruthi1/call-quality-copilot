NEXTEL COMMUNICATIONS - REFUND AND CREDIT POLICY (Rev. 4.2)

## Device return and refund window
Devices (phones, tablets, hotspots, HomeConnect routers) purchased from Nextel may be returned for a full refund within 14 calendar days of delivery or purchase. The device must be undamaged and include all original accessories. After 14 days no device refund is available; the customer may only use the manufacturer warranty. Agents must never quote a 30-day device return window.

## Refund method and timing
Approved refunds are always issued to the original payment method. Refunds are never issued as cash, gift cards, or to a different card. Card refunds post within 5 to 7 business days. Agents must not promise same-day refunds.

## Agent refund and credit authority
Tier 1 agents may approve a single refund or bill credit of up to $50.00 per account per billing cycle. Any refund or credit above $50.00 requires approval from a Tier 2 supervisor and must be handled through a warm transfer. Agents must not promise an amount above their authority before supervisor approval is obtained.

## Non-refundable items
Activation fees ($35), SIM replacement fees ($10), and prepaid top-ups are non-refundable. Used data or international add-ons are not refundable once the add-on has been consumed in whole or in part.

## Service outage credits
If a confirmed Nextel network outage affects a customer's service for more than 24 continuous hours, the customer is entitled to a prorated credit of 1/30th of the monthly plan price for each full day of the outage. The outage must be confirmed in the outage system before a credit is offered. Credits are applied to the next bill, not paid out in cash.

## Billing disputes
For disputed charges, the agent must open a billing dispute case, tell the customer the case number, and state that an investigation takes up to 10 business days. Agents may apply a courtesy credit within their authority while the dispute is open, but must not admit fault on behalf of Nextel before the investigation is complete.

## Cancellation refunds
Customers who cancel mid-cycle are billed through the end of the current billing cycle. Nextel does not prorate the final month. Agents must not tell customers they will be prorated.
